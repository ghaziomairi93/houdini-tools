Guide
How this dashboard is wired together.
Location
Set a single location; it powers weather, greeting, prayer times, and timezone-aware clocks and calendars.
Calendars & Mail
Connect Google and Microsoft once. Tokens are cached locally only (no server). You can add and delete events inline.
Tasks & Reminders
Reminders are stored in your browser under bmd_tasks. The mini calendar and pill bars keep everything in sync.
Markets
Crypto and FX/metals are manual refresh. When offline, the last successful prices are shown from local cache.
Nuke
The Nuke button clears all bmd_* keys and reloads the page. OAuth tokens are also removed.
Support
Send feedback or issues via Web3Forms.
